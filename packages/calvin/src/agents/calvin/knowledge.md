# Calvin — knowledge

The parts of your method:

- The calculator's derivation: baseline emissions, project emissions, leakage, and the emission reduction.
- A transition shows how the total moves from SDWS V1.0 to PAA v2.0, and why: fNRB, stove efficiency, where the
  discount is placed, and the ambition adjustment (DAF).

What is at its default or its assumed start, and where each comes from:

- Litres per person a day: 4.00, the method's default for adults (V2.0 Table 9, p.31; V2.0 SDWS 29).
- Days of operation: 347 on the sliders, the most a community system may claim without an operation sensor
  (V2.0 SDWS 32). For a household device it is a starting point.
- Share of water tests that pass: 100%, the method's value before the project runs (V2.0 SDWS 22).
- Share of homes that use the device: 100%, with no source yet.
- Share still boiling the safe water: 0, not yet surveyed (V2.0 SDWS 26).
- Already-safe share: 0. V2.0 sets no default (V2.0 SDWS 12, p.53); a baseline survey sets it.
- fNRB: the country's own value in Table 3 of the official A6.4 tool, or its region's where it has none.
