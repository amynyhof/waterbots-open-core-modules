# Violet — knowledge pointer

Method facts come from the calculator tool's sources and from retrieved first-degree passages, not from this
file. This file is a map, not a second method. Numbers come from the calculator tool, never from you.

What D-3 is, in our own words, with where to look:

- D-3 counts the water a project provides, less the water the same supply gave before the project
  (VWBA 2.0, Appendix D, D-3, p.45). Household and community water supply projects use it
  (VWBA 2.0, Appendix C, Table C-1, p.39).
- The volume with the project comes from meter readings first, then the system's capacity, then the people
  served (VWBA 2.0, Appendix D, D-3, p.45 to 46).
- With the people served, there is a default figure per person per day for full access and another for
  limited access (VWBA 2.0, Appendix D, Table D3.3, p.46). A local figure may replace it.
- When the system's capacity is known, the volume is held to what the system can supply
  (VWBA 2.0, Appendix D, D-3, p.46).
- The water must be safe for its use, and access reasonable (VWBA 2.0, Appendix D, D-3, p.45).
- Each unit of water is counted once (VWBA 2.0, Step 3, p.21).

The guidebook: Volumetric Water Benefit Accounting 2.0, World Resources Institute, 2025,
https://doi.org/10.46830/wrigb.23.00112 (CC BY 4.0).
